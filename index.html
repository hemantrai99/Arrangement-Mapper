Sections
Shortcuts: drag = move (neighbors make room; selected groups move together) · Shift+click or drag a box on empty timeline = multi-select · Ctrl+A = all · edges = resize · Ctrl/⌥/⌘ + drag = copy · double-click region = rename · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z = undo / redo · Space = play/stop · ←/→ nudge · Shift+←/→ resize · Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V · A–D set variant (0 clears) · Delete · click the ruler to set play start.
Zoom: Ctrl/⌘ + wheel over a timeline zooms at the cursor (the normal wheel scrolls the page) · Shift+wheel scrolls sideways. 4/4 marker (button at the far right of the bar above, or M): goes at the end of the selected region, or at the play-start bar if nothing is selected. Drag its label to move it, ✕ removes it; the 4-bar grid restarts from each marker.
